How it works

Words are counted as whitespace-separated tokens, sentences by ./!/? terminators, and paragraphs by blank-line-separated blocks. Reading time assumes 200 words/minute and speaking time assumes 130 words/minute, both common estimates. Everything runs locally in your browser as you type.
