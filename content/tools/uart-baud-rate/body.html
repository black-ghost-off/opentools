How it works

STM32 USART baud rate is derived from the peripheral clock through the 16-bit BRR register, split into a mantissa and a fraction:

baud = fCK ÷ (8 × (2 − OVER8) × USARTDIV)

With over-sampling by 16 the fraction is 4 bits (/16 steps); with over-sampling by 8 the fraction is 3 bits (/8 steps, stored left-shifted by one bit in the register). Over-sampling by 8 allows higher baud rates for a given clock at the cost of noise immunity.

AVR USARTs use a simpler 12-bit UBRRn divider:

UBRR = fOSC ÷ (8 or 16 × baud) − 1

Setting U2X = 1 (double speed) halves the divisor, trading accuracy for range the same way STM32's over-sampling-by-8 does.
